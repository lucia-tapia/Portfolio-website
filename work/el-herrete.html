El Herrete

Visual identity, web illustration and motion graphics for a creative agency.

El Herrete is an independent creative agency built on a clear conviction: brands shouldn’t just communicate more, but communicate better, with intention, coherence and a little bit of fun. Named after the aglet, the small tip at the end of a shoelace that keeps it from fraying, the agency keeps brand storytelling structured and seamless, with a humorous, upbeat atmosphere tying it all together.

As a close collaborator during the agency’s founding, I developed their visual identity and a suite of web illustrations and vector animations that anchor their digital presence. The use of sharp typography and a striking color palette combined with an approachable, friendly visual tone reflects the agency’s promise: deep strategic thinking wrapped in a light, engaging mindset.
