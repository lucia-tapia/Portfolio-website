Givenchy Archives

Learning a couture house’s illustration language by hand.

An academic study of Givenchy’s fashion illustration and the visual language behind the house’s distinctive sense of elegance.

Hubert de Givenchy founded his fashion house in 1952 and became known for architectural, timeless silhouettes. His hand-drawn illustrations served as the starting point for many of his designs, capturing mood, drape and structure with remarkable speed and precision.

To study his approach, I created twelve illustrations reinterpreting pieces from Givenchy’s archives, concluding the series with an original garment and accompanying accessories inspired by his visual language.
