Interpretation of Forms

A fashion capsule and illustration series inspired by six art movements.

This series explores the intersection of fine art and fashion design through six distinct artistic approaches: imitation, accentuation, deconstruction, stylization, abstraction, and fantasy. The challenge was to translate each style into a couture look while preserving the visual qualities that make it recognizable.

The project became an exercise in understanding how each approach uses line, shape, color, and composition, while developing my own visual interpretation of each. Each board presents the complete look alongside the garment, accessories, and visual references that informed it, brought together through mixed media with a particular focus on texture, color, and detail.

01 Imitation

Art Nouveau, after Alphonse Mucha. Organic lines and botanical motifs: a fluid green dress with a dragonfly bodice, soft pink silk draping, and gold filigree accessories shaped like curling vines and flowers.

02 Accentuation

Alien couture. Exaggerated proportions for a sharp, futuristic silhouette, drawn from Zaha Hadid’s architecture: a structured leather corset, sharp shoulders and thigh-high boots, with sheer trailing fabric and techno eyewear.

03 Deconstruction

Cubism, after Pablo Picasso. Forms broken down and put back together: an asymmetric, color-blocked dress under a mosaic cape that cracks open in the center like glass, showing the dress underneath. A Picasso-inspired face is painted on the back.

04 Stylization

Barbara Hepworth. Shapes simplified down to their essential geometry, like her organic sculptures: clean lines, wide-leg trousers, a draped top, an oversized trench and geometric gold jewelry.

05 Abstraction

Hilma af Klint. Pure color, spirals and soft geometry: a voluminous pink bubble dress with subtle embroidery, spiralled accessories and snail-shell heels.

06 Fantasy

Sirens. A mythical look inspired by deep-sea lore: a seashell top draped in strands of pearls over a layered, iridescent mermaid skirt in sheer purple and turquoise.
